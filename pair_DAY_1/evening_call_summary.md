Birkity also sent a written explainer for **my** Day 1 question (inference-time cautious vs overconfident language; Word doc *Inference-Time Mechanisms: Cautious vs Overconfident Language in LLMs*). In short, she framed it as **logits → softmax → sampling**: under weak evidence the next-token distribution flattens across semantic clusters (e.g. “immediately / this week” vs “phase / scope / discovery”), while strong prompt bias or sharp logits make one cluster dominate; **temperature and top‑p** then decide whether you almost always take the slight leader (often sounding overcommitted) or let the cautious tail get sampled; she also tied **prefill vs decode** and **attention-sink anchoring** to why prompt influence can wane as generated tokens accumulate, and suggested practical sweeps (vary T and p on the same prompt) to see the style flip—matching how I score commitment cues in my Week 11 bench.
